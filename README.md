# TBCA Reputable (TBC Anniversary)

## Summary (One-Liner)
**The ultimate lightweight reputation tracker for TBC Anniversary: Favorites, Auto-Switching, Session Stats, and Chat Alerts.**

## Description
**TBCA Reputable** is a feature-rich reputation tracker designed specifically for **WoW Classic: TBC Anniversary**. It eliminates the need to constantly check your reputation pane by providing smart auto-switching, detailed chat alerts, and a convenient "Favorites" dashboard.

### Features

- **🎯 Smart Auto-Switch**: Automatically changes your experience bar to track the faction you just gained reputation with (unless you are already Exalted).
- **⭐ Favorites System**: 
    - Check the box next to any faction in your Reputation list to mark it as a Favorite.
    - **Quick Dashboard**: Hover over the **Character Info / Micro Menu** button (bottom right) to see a summary of all your favorite factions.
- **📈 Advanced Tracking**:
    - **Real-time Stats**: Shows your current Rank, Percentage, and exact value.
    - **Session Gain**: Tracks how much reputation you've earned since logging in (e.g., `+150`).
    - **Remaining**: Displays exactly how much reputation is needed to reach the next standing (e.g., `2150 remaining`).
- **💬 Enhanced Chat**: Upon gaining reputation, prints a detailed report: `[Faction]: 1500/3000 (Friendly) - 1500 Remaining`.

### Usage

#### Managing Favorites
1. Open your Reputation Tab (press `U` or open Character Info > Reputation).
2. Click the **checkbox** to the left of any faction bar to add/remove it from favorites.

#### Viewing Favorites
Hover over the **Character Info** button (the head icon) in your main menu bar. A tooltip will appear above it listing your tracked factions.

#### Slash Commands
- `/rep fav <Faction Name>` - Add or remove a faction from favorites by name.
- `/rep list` - Print a list of your current favorites to the chat window.

### Installation
1. Download the zip file.
2. Extract the `TBCA_Reputation` folder into your `Interface\AddOns` directory.
3. Reload your UI (`/reload`).
